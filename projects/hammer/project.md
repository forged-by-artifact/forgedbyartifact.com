I came across an incredible model of Thor's hammer which I couldn't wait to 3D print. Printed in matte black, within a few hours I used dry brushing technique to bring this to life. Additional touches of leather and jute gave even more textures to this hero prop. Model credits to Oliver as @Darkwiese on Makerworld. Uncommissioned.

## Model

This model was printed overnight and utilized the maximum dimensions of the Bambu X1 Carbon. Quick turnaround and low cost.

plate_1.png

## Paint and dress

In under 24 hours, I produced this 1:1 scale set-ready hero prop from materials to finished.
