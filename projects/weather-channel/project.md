An Open House event at the local Nova Labs makerspace needed exhibits and demos. I had previously been experimenting with local RF signal broadcasting and put together a custom Nova Labs weather channel using the Weatherstar 4000+ open source project.

## Day 1 — RF Testing

Picked up and tested an RF transmitter. Local station channel 11 with NTSC video signal and audio was eventually chosen and configured.

PXL_20250604_204335224.MP~2.jpg

## Day 2 — Broadcast Sample

A laptop and a Playdate handheld game console used the Playdate's Mirror application to view the FMV Game Blippo+ via the RF transmitter to a black and white TV.

PXL_20250607_182129726.MP.jpg

## Day 3 — Raspberry Pi Portable Board

A raspberry pi and the RF transmitter and a power strip were mounted to a pegboard so the broadcast system could be easily moved for exhibit displays.

PXL_20251013_002752105.jpg

## Day 4 - Open House

The open source project Weatherstar 4000+ was hosted on github pages and the raspberry pi acts as a kiosk to view it. A display at Nova Labs makerspace open house was prepared to broadcast it to multiple vintage TVs over the air.
